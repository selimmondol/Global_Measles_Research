## Data availability

The original raw datasets and processed model-input datasets are currently excluded from this repository.

The repository provides research scripts, recorded analytical outputs, country-year predictions, and the frozen evaluation protocol.

The verified independent-test reproduction used locally supplied original input datasets.

## Reuse conditions

The World Bank generally makes its open datasets available under CC BY 4.0, subject to dataset-specific terms.

WHO and WHO/UNICEF data reuse must follow the applicable conditions of the specific source and download.

No independent redistribution permission is asserted here for the WHO source files or derived processed datasets.
