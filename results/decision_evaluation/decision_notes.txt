VALIDATION ONLY: 2019–2021. Test and unseen-country partitions not accessed.
Annual top-K selects CEIL(10% or 20% of eligible countries) separately each year.
Pooled recall = sum detected / sum events across years; not average annual recall.
Tie breaking: descending score, ascending country name.
Coverage-shortfall ranking uses 100 - min(previous-year MCV1, MCV2);
missing coverage is ranked last, not assigned coverage=0. This is a simple
research comparator, NOT the WHO risk-assessment framework.
Fixed <95% coverage screen uses only countries with both vaccines observed;
no imputation of vaccine coverage for this rule. Capacity is not fixed.
Per-year target is a researcher-defined annual incidence surge, NOT
WHO-defined outbreak onset. Reported cases are surveillance-dependent.
All 2019–2021 comparisons are validation/exploratory, used repeatedly in
development. Do not claim unbiased out-of-sample improvement or significance.
Present-day WUENIC revisions do not imply historical real-time availability.
Country-year observations across calendar years are dependent.
Selection by validation performance must be frozen before 2022–2025 test.
