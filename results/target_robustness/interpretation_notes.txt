TARGET ROBUSTNESS — PROVISIONAL REPORTED INCIDENCE SURGES ONLY
Primary candidate C: incidence >= 5 per million, >= 2x prior three-year median,
and increase >= 0.5 per million. This is a researcher-defined threshold,
NOT a WHO outbreak definition and NOT yet a final prespecified endpoint.
Zero-baseline sensitivity excludes zero-median rows; it does not recode them as 0.
Strict onset needs a verified previous-year negative label; an unknown previous
year is neither assumed negative nor counted as a strict onset.
Consecutive positive labels are not independent outbreaks.
Threshold grid is descriptive and not intended for cherry-picking results.
High MCV: national prior-year WUENIC >=95% for both doses; not true immunity.
Reported incidence depends on surveillance completeness. Revised historical
WUENIC estimates do not establish what was available in each historical year.
No models trained; original files and labels unchanged.
